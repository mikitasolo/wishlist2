# Domain invariants

These are correctness/privacy rules the product depends on; AGENTS.md links here, and they must not regress.

## Roles & ownership

- **Two kinds of people: owners and members.** No `admin` tier: anyone trusted to manage a list is a co-owner. Owners are `Wishlist.owners[]`; members are `WishlistMember` rows with **no role field** (membership is existence, not a level).
- **All co-owners are equal.** Identical authority (deleting the list included), the list shows on each one's profile, none ever sees booking status. Authorize owners only via `isWishlistOwner` / `ownedByFilter` / `primaryOwnerId` (`lib/utils/wishlistOwners.ts`) or `getOwnerAccess` / `isOwnerOfWishlist` (`wishlistAccess.ts`), never a hand-rolled check on `owners[]`. A list always keeps an owner: take one off only via `removeWishlistOwner` (`lib/utils/wishlistOwnership.ts`), whose single conditional write refuses the last owner and an actor no longer an owner; no side effect unless it returns `'removed'`. An `isLastOwner` check or an owner check against an earlier read is only a pre-check: it races (two co-owners leaving, two owners removing each other), and the schema's non-empty validator doesn't run on `updateOne`. Deleting a list is one `findOneAndDelete` (`deleteOwnedWishlist`, `lib/utils/wishlistDeletion.ts`), editing it (settings and the `featured` pin together) one `findOneAndUpdate`, each filtered on `ownedByFilter(callerId)`; nothing else is touched unless it matched. Where writes are on other collections (kick, invite, promote), re-read ownership right before the first change; that only narrows the race, so prefer a conditional write whenever the write is on the `Wishlist`. A caller who lost ownership mid-request gets the same answer as any non-owner.
- **Members have no management powers.** They browse, propose items and book gifts; they can't see the members list, drafts or settings, or publish an item directly. Everything else is owner-only.
- **Becoming an owner requires consent and releases your bookings.** Three ways in: create the list, accept an `owner` invite (`POST /api/wishlists/[id]/members` by username), or accept a promotion (`POST /api/wishlists/[id]/owners/[userId]`, which issues the same kind of invite, never a silent role write). `/members` honours the invitee's `whoCanInvite`; promotion skips it, so it goes only to an **existing member** (404 for anyone else, identical whether or not the user exists). On accept, `addWishlistOwner` frees their booked items **and deletes their reservations** (an owner can't be their own gifter) — the one membership change that deletes a reservation; leave/remove keep it (see "A booking must never be left…"). **A share link never makes anyone an owner**: joining through a Join link (WIS-337) only ever adds a member (the joiner's consent is the confirmation, the owners' is having made the link), and since that can race a grant, the join re-reads `owners`, `archived` and the kick block after its membership upsert and deletes only the row it created if they became an owner meanwhile (`addWishlistOwner` / `promoteMemberToOwner`), the list was archived or they were kicked; a pending `owner` invite stays pending.
- **Being demoted, removed or kicked tells you, and only you.** Demotion sends a `role_changed` notification (linking to the list); removing an owner or kicking a member sends `removed_from_wishlist` (no link; the list may be hidden from them now). Deleting a list notifies nobody and clears its notifications; `createNotification(s)` (`lib/notifications.ts`) takes back one that lands after its list is gone, so write a list's notifications only through them. Each goes out only once its write landed, never for a refused or lost one, never for leaving. Neither names the acting owner (`createNotification` drops the actor for both types, as for proposals), mentions bookings, or reaches the other owners. **A kick also keeps them out of the list's share links (WIS-338)**: see Share links. When a co-owner is removed or made a member, the links stay; if the list has live ones, the owner who did it is then offered `RevokeShareLinksDialog` (each live link with Revoke, and Done), a client-side follow-up the removal doesn't wait for.
- **Occasion reminders go to a list's members only, once per occurrence (WIS-193).** The daily cron `GET /api/cron/occasion-reminders` (`sendOccasionReminders`, `lib/utils/occasionReminders.ts`) writes one `occasion_reminder` 7 days before an unarchived list's next occurrence (catch-up: up to 2 days later, after a missed or capped run; counted from the UTC date), to its `WishlistMember`s as they are at send time: never its owners (excluded even with a stray member row), never friends who could merely see it, never anyone with `notificationPrefs.occasion_reminder.inApp: false` (missing = on). Once per person, list and occurrence: a unique partial index on `Notification { user, wishlist, occasionDate }` (`type: 'occasion_reminder'`), written as upserts by `createOccasionReminders` (`lib/notifications.ts`), so re-runs and overlapping runs add nothing. After the write it re-reads the list and its people and takes back the rows it just wrote unless they still hold (list gone, archived, occasion moved → all; no longer a member, now an owner, opted out → theirs); it never deletes an earlier run's row. At most `OCCASION_REMINDER_MAX_PER_RUN` (500) new rows a run, nearest occasion first, the rest carried over. The row names the list, the occasion's kind and its day (`occasionDate`, a real next occurrence, never a yearly one's stored year): no actor, no item, no count, nothing about bookings. **The email** (WIS-321, `sendReminderEmails`) goes to the same audience in the same window by its own setting (`occasion_reminder.email`, missing = on), independent of the in-app one: once per person, list and occurrence through a claim taken first under `NotificationEmailClaim`'s unique index (`claimOccasionReminderEmail`), then the list and person are re-read (gone, archived, moved, left, owner now, opted out → the claim is given back, nothing sent); a failed send gives the claim back for the next run. At most `OCCASION_EMAIL_MAX_PER_RUN` (50) a run.
- **Reminders about what you've reserved go only to whoever holds it, as a plain member (WIS-384).** Two events of their own (operator decision 2026-10-10), never folded into the occasion reminder: `gift_reminder`, 7 days before an unarchived list's next occurrence, and `gift_nudge`, `GIFT_NUDGE_DELAY_DAYS` (3) after one, only about reservations made before its day was over; each with the occasion reminders' catch-up (up to 2 days late, counted from the UTC date). The daily cron `GET /api/cron/gift-reminders` (06:30 UTC, `sendGiftReminders`, `lib/utils/giftReminders.ts`). **Who** (`giftHolders`, per event): someone with a `Reservation` on the list that is theirs to settle, who is a plain member now (a membership, not an owner, the list there and not archived). Before: its item still `booked` by them alone (no group gift), or its item's live group gift still has them as a contributor. After, what they can mark as given: a solo booking as above, one whose item an owner has since marked received (`completed`: received isn't given), or a live group gift they **organize** (only the organizer gives it, for everyone; the other contributors would get a 409, so aren't nudged). The list and memberships are read before the items, so a promotion (grant, then release) or a leave (membership delete, then free) landing in between is seen by one of the two reads. Marking a gift as given ends its Reservation, so the nudge only reaches what isn't given; a kept Reservation whose item was released, drafted or deleted, or one kept after a leave or kick, holds nothing. Never owners, never other members, never anyone with `notificationPrefs.<event>.inApp: false`. Once per person, list and occurrence per event: unique partial indexes on `Notification { user, wishlist, occasionDate }` per type (`gift_reminder_once`, `gift_nudge_once`), written as upserts by `createGiftReminders`; after the write the list and each recipient are read again and the rows just written are taken back unless they still hold (list gone, archived or its occasion moved → all; no longer holding, made owner, left, kicked, opted out → theirs; a failed re-check takes them all back); never an earlier run's. At most `GIFT_REMINDER_MAX_PER_RUN` (500) new rows a run per event, the earliest occurrence first. **The feed re-checks it on every read**: `GET /api/notifications` (and its mark-all-read) hides, and doesn't count, each gift reminder row the reader no longer holds what it's about for, checked row by row by its own event and occurrence (`hiddenGiftReminderRows`: a nudge is never revived by a reservation made after its occasion), leaving them in place; a reader with no such row costs one read. The row names the list, the occasion's kind and day and links to Reserved (`/booked`): no item, no count, no actor, nothing about anyone else's reservations. No spoiler mode hides it like a group gift row (`hiddenByDiscreetMode`). **The email**, per event its own setting (**off by default**, orchestrator default awaiting the operator), the same audience and window, once per person, list and occurrence through a `NotificationEmailClaim` (`kind`: the type, `key`: the occurrence's date) claimed first (`claimOccurrenceEmail`), then the list, the holding and the setting re-read (any change → the claim is given back, nothing sent); a failed send gives it back. At most `GIFT_EMAIL_MAX_PER_RUN` (30) a run, both events. Account deletion removes the rows and claims with the person's others. `test/api/giftReminders.test.ts`.
- **The one exception: a friend's birthday reminder is about a person, not a list (WIS-345).** Occasion reminders above stay members-only; this is a separate reminder, `birthday_reminder`, about someone's profile birthday (`User.birthday`). The daily birthday cron `GET /api/cron/birthdays` (its `reminders` task, `sendBirthdayReminders` in `lib/utils/birthdayReminders.ts`, within its own time budget: `lib/utils/birthdayCron.ts`) writes one 7 days before a person's next birthday (the occasion reminders' lead and catch-up, `reminderDayOffsets`, counted from the UTC date), to each of their **friends** (mutual follows) as they are at send time, and only while the birthday helper admits a friend (`birthdayVisibleFilter('friend')` picks the people, `canSeeBirthday(privacy, 'friend')` decides each: `nobody` sends nothing; a friends-only profile still reaches friends). Never a one-way follower, never the person, never anyone with `notificationPrefs.birthday_reminder.inApp: false` (missing = on). **No double reminder**: a friend who is a member (`WishlistMember`) of any of the person's own unarchived lists of occasion kind `birthday` whose next occurrence is within the same reminder window from the run's date (`listsDueForReminder`, narrowed to hers) **and within `BIRTHDAY_LIST_COVER_DAYS` (7) either side of her birthday** (`listCoversBirthday`, WIS-347: a child's or partner's list she co-owns on another day covers nobody) is skipped on each channel their `occasion_reminder` setting is on for (`coveredByBirthdayLists`): that list's reminder covers them there (one who turned occasion reminders off in app still gets the birthday one in app); several lists are all checked; an archived list, one outside the window, another kind, or a list the person doesn't own skips nobody, and a co-owner is no member. Once per friend, person and occurrence: a unique partial index on `Notification { user, actor, occasionDate }` (`type: 'birthday_reminder'`), written as upserts by `createBirthdayReminders`; after the write it re-reads the person and the audience and takes back the rows it just wrote unless they still hold (birthday cleared, moved or hidden from friends → all; unfriended, now covered by a Birthday list, opted out → theirs); never an earlier run's. **The feed re-checks it on every read**: `GET /api/notifications` (the badge's poll too) first deletes, among the reader's birthday reminders it could show or count (unread ones, and those among the newest `FEED_LIMIT` rows, looked at again when a deletion brings an older one in), those they may no longer see (`dropHiddenBirthdayReminders`: person gone, birthday cleared, moved or hidden from friends, no longer friends; a reader with no birthday reminder at all costs one existence check, and with none to check it reads nothing more), so a hidden birthday never stays on screen or in the unread count, and names the rest by the person's username now (by `actor`, never the written one). It admits **row by row** (WIS-347): only a row it checked itself shows, never one because another row about the same person passed; if that re-check fails, every birthday reminder is left out of both the rows and the unread count. At most `BIRTHDAY_REMINDER_MAX_PER_RUN` (500) new rows a run, nearest birthday first. The row (`actor`: the person, `actorUsername`: their handle, `occasionDate`: the real next day) reads "@anna’s birthday is on March 14" and links to their profile: never a list (no `wishlist*` fields), no year, no item, no count, nothing about bookings or who else was reminded. **The email** has its own setting (`birthday_reminder.email`, **off by default**: operator's decision), the same audience and window, once per friend, person and occurrence through a `NotificationEmailClaim` (`kind: 'birthday_reminder'`, no list, `about`: the person, `key`: `<personId>:<date>`) claimed first (`claimBirthdayReminderEmail`), then everything re-read (birthday, friendship, Birthday-list cover, setting); who is pending on either channel is picked from one read per person per run of their friends and covering lists' members; a failed or refused send gives the claim back. At most `BIRTHDAY_EMAIL_MAX_PER_RUN` (50) a run. Account deletion removes the rows and claims to and about the person.
- **Losing ownership clears the per-owner state.** Demote, remove or leave pulls the user from `owners` **and** `featuredBy`. Only owners change ownership; nobody changes their own role.
- **"Featured" is per-owner** (`Wishlist.featuredBy[]`): pinning touches nobody else's dashboard. Responses fold it to the caller's boolean `featured` via `serializeWishlist`. On the list's page it's **Pin to my dashboard** in the header's ⋯ menu (`components/WishlistHeader.tsx`) beside Leave list, never in the settings form. On the dashboard and Your wishlists it's **Pin to top** (WIS-252): a round pin button over the cover of each of your own active lists' cards and at the end of its phone row (`PinButton`, via `WishlistList`'s `pins`, never on a shared or archived list), toggled by `usePinToTop` with the same `PATCH { featured }`. The pinned list sorts first (`pinnedFirst`, `lib/utils/pinnedLists.ts`, over the occasion order); its pin button is the only sign (no Pinned badge, WIS-302): always named **Pin to top**, `aria-pressed` for state (tests: `getByRole('button', { name: 'Pin to top', pressed })`), filled while pinned, tooltip saying what pressing does (Pin to top / Unpin). The server keeps one pin per owner (pinning unpins their other), and the page's copy follows (`withPin`). It orders only your own screens.

## Invites & join requests

- **Leaving a list or losing a role on it cancels your pending invites for it.** Accepting grants the invite's role and can't refuse non-members (`owner` invites legitimately go to them), so a leftover one would let e.g. a kicked member accept a pending promotion as co-owner. Leave, kick, remove-owner and demote call `cancelPendingInvites` (`lib/utils/wishlistInvites.ts`). An owner write (remove-owner, demote, a co-owner leaving) calls it only once the write landed, never when `removeWishlistOwner` refused it (a co-owner's leave refused because a demotion just made them a member proceeds as that member's leave). A kick or member leave calls it both before and after its membership delete, and answers 404, not success, when that delete matched nothing. It deletes only that person's _pending_ invites for the list (accepted/declined rows stay) plus, best-effort, their `wishlist_invite` notifications for it.
- **An owner taken off the owners loses the invites they sent; one who leaves doesn't.** Once `removeWishlistOwner` returned `'removed'`, remove-owner and demote also call `cancelInvitesSentBy` (`lib/utils/wishlistInvites.ts`): each of that owner's _pending_ invites for the list, paused included, in its own conditional delete on the pending row, so it races an accept like an owner's cancel (an already-claimed invite stays claimed and that accept completes). Invitees' `wishlist_invite` notifications go best-effort (`clearCancelledInviteNotifications`, also an owner's cancel's). A co-owner leaving of their own accord keeps theirs (any owner can cancel them). Since that cleanup reads before deleting, both invite routes (`POST /members`, `POST /owners/[userId]`) re-check the inviter's ownership once the invite exists (`withdrawUnlessInviterOwns`), else withdraw it and answer as to any non-owner — so an invite issued while its inviter leaves is withdrawn too. An accept that reopens its claim (failed grant, list archived under it) is likewise withdrawn once reopened if its inviter no longer owns the list, unless the grant already made the invitee an owner. **`invite_accepted` never reaches a former owner**: it goes to the inviter only while they still own the list (read once the role is granted), else to every current owner but the invitee (`createNotifications`).
- **An invite is answered by claiming it, before anything is granted.** Accept/decline record the answer in one conditional write on the pending invite; no match → 404, unless a read after it finds the invitee's own answer already there (WIS-100): then 409 with its `status` (an accepted one only while they hold its role, since an accepted invite outlives a leave/kick and a claim being granted may be reopened), plus for an accepted one the list's link (`wishlistSlug`, `wishlistId`) only while `getWishlistViewAccess` lets them open it; `usePendingInvites` drops the card and says "You've already joined “X”" (Open list if linked) or "You already declined this invitation". Anyone else's invite, answered or not, an answered one on a list archived to the caller, or one whose list is gone gets the `Invite not found` 404 (the deleted list's 404 for the last). Once the answer landed (an accept's once the role is granted), `recordInviteAnswer` (`lib/notifications.ts`) writes it onto the invitee's `wishlist_invite` notification and marks it read, best-effort ("You joined …" / "You declined the invite to …"), picking the row by time (written between invite and answer; notifications carry no invite id). An owner's cancel, `cancelPendingInvites` and `cancelInvitesSentBy` are conditional deletes on the same pending row, so exactly one racer lands. A failed grant reopens the claim (`pending`); accepting again finishes. A promotion invite carries `promotion: true` (set only by `POST /owners/[userId]`); accepting it, or any `owner` invite to a member, goes through `promoteMemberToOwner` (`lib/utils/wishlistOwnership.ts`), which takes the membership row in one conditional delete before granting. Kick and leave delete that row, so removal and promotion can't both land; if it's already gone, nothing is granted and the claimed invite is withdrawn (`withdrawInvite`): a promotion never makes a non-member an owner. A promotion also re-checks membership once its invite exists and withdraws it with the same 404 if the member has gone (not if they already accepted, which takes the row too). An accept that loses to a list delete grants nothing: the delete removes the list before its memberships, so joining checks the list _after_ its membership upsert and takes the row back if it's gone. Adding or proposing an item does likewise (WIS-182): `POST /api/wishlists/[id]/items` re-reads the list after writing and, if gone, takes the item back, frees its image (a copy's copied blob included) and answers the same 404. `addWishlistOwner` / `promoteMemberToOwner` return `null` when their ownership write matched no list and skip the booking release (it would destroy reservations the delete keeps). Either way the claimed invite is withdrawn and the answer is 404 `This wishlist no longer exists`.
- **Invites are registered-users-only** (by username). No email-invite path; invites match `invitedUser`.
- **A group invite is only many single invites (WIS-37).** `POST /api/wishlists/[id]/members/group` (`{ groupId, role }`, owners only, the People tab's Invite → Group) reads the group only via `groupMemberIds` (someone else's, a missing one and a malformed id are the same `Group not found` 404) and issues one ordinary invite per person through `issueWishlistInvite`, each held to every rule `POST /members` applies through the same checks (`lib/utils/inviteChecks.ts`: `whoCanInviteRefusal`, `listStandingRefusal`, `issueInvite`, which both routes call, so they can't drift), worded the same (`INVITE_REFUSAL`, `lib/utils/wishlistInvites.ts`): someone already an owner, a member or with a pending invite is skipped without spending a token (the owner sees all three on People anyway); everyone else spends one `invite` rate-limit token before their `whoCanInvite` is read, and once the limit runs dry the rest are only counted (`notInvited`), never tried; the cap answers per person. An archived list refuses a `member` group invite whole (409), after the ownership re-read. Each issued invite is re-checked with `withdrawUnlessInviterOwns`; if the caller lost the list, every invite the request issued is withdrawn and it answers as to a non-owner. The answer names people only as the group's owner already sees them (`publicDisplayName`, never an email). Nothing links the group to the list afterwards. Tests: `test/api/groupInvites.test.ts`.
- **One pending invite per person per list, and an issued invite never hinges on its email.** Issue invites only via `issueWishlistInvite` (`lib/utils/wishlistInvites.ts`). A unique partial index on `WishlistInvite { wishlist, invitedUser }` (`status: 'pending'`) refuses a concurrent duplicate, answered "already pending" with no second email or notification; partial so answered rows stay and a declined invitee can be re-invited. Once the invite exists its notification is created regardless; a failed email is caught, logged, and doesn't fail the request. **Nobody has more than 20 pending invites waiting** (WIS-40, `PENDING_INVITE_CAP`), across all inviters and lists, counting only pending ones from the last 30 days (`PENDING_INVITE_CAP_WINDOW_MS`; older ones stay pending and visible but don't count) that the invitee is shown and can answer: paused (`isInvitePaused`) or on a gone list doesn't count, or archiving could fill someone's cap behind their back. (Unarchiving resumes paused ones, so they may briefly see more than 20: the cap bounds new invites.) At the cap `issueWishlistInvite` returns `'capped'` having created and sent nothing, and every invite path answers 429 `This person has too many pending invitations right now` (`pendingInviteCapResponse`); a join request's Invite puts the request back. It's checked after "already pending" and after every pre-issue refusal (`whoCanInvite` included), so only someone who could otherwise invite learns of it. Once the invite exists the others are recounted and it's withdrawn while still pending if they already fill the cap: racers may all be refused, but the cap is never exceeded.
- **An archived list's pending `member` invites are paused** (`isInvitePaused`, `lib/utils/wishlistInvites.ts`): joining would leave the invitee on a list they can't open. They stay pending but are left out of the invitee's `GET /api/users/me/invites` and notifications (`pausedInviteWishlistIds`: feed, unread count and mark-all-read skip the unanswered `wishlist_invite` row, which isn't deleted). Accepting or declining one answers the `Invite not found` 404 without claiming it (indistinguishable from missing to a non-owner; declinable once unarchived), and an owner issuing one gets 409 `Unarchive this list to invite members` (checked last). Accept re-reads the list after writing the membership row; if archived meanwhile, it deletes the row it created (never one already held) and reopens the claim, same 404. If that row is already gone, a kick took it: the invite is withdrawn instead. If a newer invite took the person's pending slot while the claim held it (a promotion of the brief member, WIS-294), the claim can't reopen: the claimed row is deleted with its own notification (picked by time, never `withdrawInvite`, which would take the newer one's too), and a promotion that took the slot is withdrawn, its membership being gone. Unarchiving resumes them all. `owner` invites, promotions included, work as on any list.
- **A request to join grants nothing, and only a list you can open takes one (WIS-275).** A signed-in non-member, non-owner whom `getWishlistViewAccess` admits gets **Ask to join** in the "Want to reserve a gift?" callout (`JoinListCallout`, `useJoinRequest`): it records a `WishlistJoinRequest` (`POST /api/wishlists/[id]/requests`) and notifies every owner via `createNotifications` (`join_request`, "@ivan asked to join …", public handle, linking to People). The requester's routes (that `POST`, `GET` / `DELETE /requests/me`) go through `readListForRequester` (`lib/utils/joinRequestAccess.ts`, by `admitListViewer`, so a live share link to the list lets anyone holding it ask, whatever its visibility, WIS-335), answering every refusal (archived, private, friends-only to a non-friend, a dead link) with the missing list's `Wishlist not found` 404 (a cancel still removes their own request from a list hidden since, or whose link died, behind that 404); `joinRequest` rate-limits only once the caller may ask, with the new-account tier. One pending request per person per list (unique partial index on `{ wishlist, user }`, `status: 'pending'`; a concurrent duplicate is answered as already requested, notifying nobody). Owners answer under **Requests** on People (`GET /requests`, `PATCH /requests/[requestId]`, via `getOwnerAccess`, listed in `test/api/archivedOwnerRoutes.test.ts`): **Invite** issues a normal `member` invite via `issueWishlistInvite` (requester's `whoCanInvite` ignored; re-reads the inviter's ownership, refuses on an archived list as `POST /members` does, spends `invite`), which the requester must still accept; **Decline** keeps the row `declined`, telling nobody. Both re-read the caller's ownership right before writing. Each answer claims the pending row in one conditional write, so exactly one of Invite, Decline and the requester's cancel lands; an Invite whose invite is then withdrawn (`withdrawUnlessInviterOwns`) puts the request back. Decline is silent: for `ASK_AGAIN_AFTER_MS` (30 days, `lib/utils/joinRequests.ts`) `GET /requests/me` keeps answering `requested`, a cancel only marks the declined row `withdrawn`, and asking again only clears that, with no notification; after it an ask is new. A request never outlives its subject: list delete deletes its requests (`deleteOwnedWishlist`; an ask landing after takes its row back), account delete deletes them (`deleteAccount`), joining through any invite ends it (`endPendingJoinRequest`, which also marks a declined one `withdrawn` so it doesn't read Requested once they're off the list again), as do leave and kick (via `cancelPendingInvites`); the owners' list omits anyone already on the list. Ending one clears the owners' `join_request` notifications about it (`clearJoinRequestNotifications`). Members and owners are never offered Ask to join; their `/requests/me` never reads requested.
- **Asking a friend for a wishlist grants nothing and never reveals a list (WIS-324).** On a friend's profile showing no list, **Ask for a wishlist** (`AskForWishlist`) calls `POST /api/users/[id]/ask`: only a friend (mutual follow; anyone else 403) who can open none of the person's lists (`canOpenAnyListOf`, each list through `getWishlistViewAccess`; one they can open → 409), rate-limited only once they may ask (`wishlistAsk`, new-account tier). One `WishlistAsk` row per (asker, person) under a unique index, written by one conditional upsert (`askForWishlist`, `lib/utils/wishlistAsks.ts`): an ask holds for `WISHLIST_ASK_WINDOW_MS` (30 days) from when it was made, answered or not (so hiding the list that answered it can't renew it), a second ask meanwhile is answered as asked and tells nobody, and there's no decline (it quietly expires). It's live for telling the asker until answered. Once written, friendship and lists are re-checked and the ask taken back (403 / 409) if either changed, unless a new list already answered it (then it went through). The person gets `wishlist_ask` ("@ivan wants to know what you’d like", Activity's **Create a list**), unless their own budget (`wishlistAskReceived`, keyed on them) is spent, which holds back only the notification, so the asker learns nothing about other asks. `GET /api/users/[id]/ask` answers only the caller's own ask ("Asked", for the window). **When the person makes a list** (`POST /api/wishlists`, `/copy`), `notifyAskersOfNewList` tells each asker with a live ask whom `getWishlistViewAccess` lets open it, claiming the ask first (so nobody is told twice), with `asked_list_made` ("@anna made a wishlist you asked for: …"); then re-reads the list and takes the row back, sends no email and gives the ask back to anyone who can no longer open it. An asker who can't open the list is told nothing and keeps their ask. Nothing about one asker reaches another or the person beyond their own ask's row. Both notifications' emails are off by default. Account deletion deletes asks made and received.

## Items, bookings & gifts

- **Owners never see booking status.** For an owner `booked` folds into `available`; never reveal who booked what. Every item an API returns is built by `serializeItem(item, viewer, rates)` (`lib/utils/itemSerializer.ts`, WIS-110) — list read (`GET /api/wishlists/[id]/items`), create (`POST`) and edit (`PATCH /api/items/[id]`) alike, so a write never shows what the read hides. The route picks the role (`owner`, `member`, `proposer` for a non-member editing their own proposal, else `visitor`): non-members get `booked` as `available` and no `bookedBy`; nobody gets another person's id in `bookedBy` / `proposedBy`; `givenByMe` (from `readGivenByViewer`) only on a member's answer, only on their own booking. New item fields/enrichments go there, never into one route. The item actions behind `PATCH` / `DELETE /api/items/[id]` live in `lib/utils/itemActions.ts` (`updateItem`, `deleteItem`: the conditional writes in order, side effects, `serializeItem`-shaped answers) and `lib/utils/itemUpdatePlan.ts` (`planItemUpdate`: who may change what, and each write's guard; pure), WIS-373; the route only authenticates, reads the item and list, authorizes, rate-limits and parses. Tests: `test/unit/itemUpdatePlan.test.ts`, `test/api/itemActions.test.ts`. **Bulk item actions (WIS-386)** go through the same two helpers: `POST /api/wishlists/[id]/items/bulk` (owners only via `getOwnerAccess`, archived lists included; `{ action, itemIds }`, at most `BULK_ITEMS_MAX` (100)) applies Publish, Move to draft, Mark as received, Mark as / Not most wanted or Delete to each item with `updateItem` / `deleteItem` (`applyBulkItemAction`, `lib/utils/bulkItemActions.ts`), so a delete ends group gifts, unlinks partners and frees the image while gifters keep their `Reservation`s, exactly as one delete does. Which items an action applies to is decided by status alone with `booked` counted as `available` (`BULK_ACTION_STATUSES`, `lib/utils/bulkItems.ts`; never a proposal, which only a review changes), and the answer (`done` / `skipped` / `gone` / `failed` ids plus owner-shaped `items`) never gives a reason, so it reads the same whether an item was reserved or not. One `Idempotency-Key` per batch, required: an `ItemBatch` row (`models/ItemBatch.ts`, unique on the scoped key, bound to a digest of list, action and ids, expiring after a day) is claimed before anything is applied; a retry gets the first answer (items re-read), 409 while that attempt runs, 422 for the key with another change; a row unfinished after `ITEM_BATCH_STALE_MS` (90 s, past the route's 60 s `maxDuration`) is dropped and the batch applied again (every action is safe to repeat). Items are changed four at a time, answered in the order sent. One `bulkItems` token (20 an hour) and one share-card purge per batch. Tests: `test/api/bulkItems.test.ts`. **Move to another list (WIS-434)** is the bulk `move` (`targetWishlistId` required, and only there): a copy onto the target and a delete here, with no second release or copy path (`applyBulkMove`, `lib/utils/bulkItemMove.ts`). The target must be another list the caller owns that isn't archived (`isMoveTarget`), checked once the key is claimed and re-checked on both lists after the copies are written; any other target is one 422 (`MOVE_TARGET_ERROR`), whatever's wrong with it, and a failed re-check takes every copy back. It takes drafts and live items (`booked` with `available`, never a received item or a proposal); each own-blob image is copied into the caller's uploads first (`copyBlobsToUploads`, shared with `/copy`; one that can't be keeps its item where it is, `failed`), copies start unreserved with links among the moved items remapped (`remapLinks`), and each source is then deleted by `deleteItem`, which frees its image only now. An item another owner deleted meanwhile, or whose delete fails, has its copy taken back (through `deleteItem` too), and so does every item still undeleted at `MOVE_DEADLINE_MS` (35 s, inside the route's 60 s and the page's 45 s). The copies are recorded on the `ItemBatch` row (`moves`, `copiedUrls`) before they're written, so an attempt that throws is settled before the route lets go of the row, and one that died is settled on a retry of its key, the caller's next move, or the daily sweep of everyone's (WIS-443: `settleAllDeadMoves`, run first by `GET /api/cron/gift-reminders` on its own budget, unfinished rows with `moves` / `copiedUrls` older than `ITEM_BATCH_STALE_MS`, oldest first, `DEAD_MOVE_SWEEP_MAX_ROWS` a run, each dropped only if still the same unfinished row; a move's row lives a week, `ITEM_BATCH_MOVE_TTL_MS`, so the sweep always reaches it) (`settleDeadMove`: a copy whose item is still here taken back, one whose item is gone kept, unused image copies freed). Each moved item's delete first re-reads the target: one deleted since the re-check leaves the item here (`failed`); a delete in the moment between that read and the item's is the same as moving then deleting the list. The answer is a delete's (moved ids `done`, only skipped items shaped), and the dialog's warning (`MOVE_WARNING`, `lib/utils/confirmationCopy.ts`) is the same whatever the items are and never says a reservation goes. Tests: `test/api/bulkMove.test.ts`.
- **"N items" means a list's published items, on every screen.** Available, booked and completed, booked folded in for everyone, so no count reveals reservations. Count with `lib/utils/itemCounts.ts` (`isPublishedItem`, `tallyItems`, formatters) or server-side `countWishlistItems` (`lib/utils/wishlistItemCounts.ts`), never your own status filter. Drafts and proposals are counted only for an owner on an owner surface ("11 items · 2 drafts · 1 to review": `draftCount` / `proposalCount`, which `GET /api/wishlists` puts on your own lists only), so a member never sees a draft or proposal counted. The share card counts the same set (`isCountedOnShareCard`), so changing the set changes which writes must purge it. `test/api/itemCounts.test.ts` checks all sources agree. Totals across your lists exclude archived ones (`/items` unless asked).
- **A booking must never be left where nobody can release it.** Only the holder can cancel a booking and owners are booking-blind, so becoming an owner must free their bookings first, or the item stays claimed forever. Route role transitions through `releaseHoldingsForRole` (`lib/utils/wishlistOwnership.ts`): → owner frees items **and** cancels reservations; → member does nothing. Leave/remove free the items but keep the reservation (the gifter can still log the gift). Demotion and visibility changes need nothing (members keep access at every tier; only members book). In flight: the item route reads the caller as member at the start, so after a member's booking writes the item and its Reservation, `undoBookingUnlessMember` (`lib/utils/lateBooking.ts`) re-reads their role; if they've left, been removed, become an owner, or the list was archived, it frees the item and ends the Reservation it wrote (restoring one it overwrote, unless they're now an owner) and refuses the request. That only closes the race because becoming an owner grants ownership before releasing, and a member's leave/kick frees their bookings again after the membership delete (`releaseLateBookings`). Any new way off a list or into its owners keeps this order.
- **Group gifts always have a holder who can release them, and owners never see them (WIS-361).** Several members split one item (pledges only, honor system; `models/GroupGift.ts`, every write through `lib/utils/groupGifts.ts`). Only list members take part, admitted as booking admits them (`admitGiftCaller`, `lib/utils/groupGiftRoutes.ts`: an owner gets one fixed 400 before anything about the item's gift is read, archived → 404, non-member → 403, the `booking` limit with its new-account tier). Routes, all under `/api/items/[id]/group-gift`: `POST` start (on an `available` item; target defaults to its price, integer cents, uppercase currency checked against the rates; `Idempotency-Key`), `PUT /me` join or change pledge, `DELETE /me` leave, and the organizer's `POST /cancel` (item released, every contributor's Reservation deleted), `/convert` (the organizer's own solo booking; everyone else's Reservation deleted) and `/given` (a `GiftLog` per contributor recording their pledge, Reservations taken into them, the item left booked by the organizer as any given gift; `Idempotency-Key` on all three). **Shape**: a live gift's item is `booked`, `bookedBy` = the organizer and `Item.groupGift` = the gift (one live gift per item, a unique partial index on `active`); each contributor holds their own `Reservation`. The gift is the source of truth: each change is one conditional write on it (live, caller still organizer/contributor) that also computes what follows (`funded` ⇔ pledges ≥ target, the next organizer, the end), bumping `rev`; the item follows through `syncGiftItem`, which never puts back an older organizer (`groupGiftRev`) and only touches an item still holding that gift. **Handover**: anyone leaving a gift, the list (leave, kick, co-owner removal), its members (promotion) or the app (account deletion) is taken out by `withdrawFromListGroupGifts`, inside `releaseUserBookings` / `releaseGifterHoldings` and so on both sides of the membership delete and after the ownership grant (the grant-then-release order is unchanged); an organizer's role passes to the earliest remaining contributor, who then holds the item; with nobody left the gift ends (`emptied`) and the item is released. Every other release skips a gift's item in its own filter (`groupGift: null`: `releaseUserBookings`, `releaseGifterHoldings`, `undoBookingUnlessMember`), and a plain unbook of one is refused (`GROUP_GIFT_UNBOOK_ERROR`). Becoming an owner deletes that person's contribution Reservation like any other; leave/kick keep it. A start or join re-checks the caller with `lostMemberReason` after its writes and takes itself back (`withdrawFromGift` + `syncGiftItem`) if they left, were removed, became an owner or the list was archived meanwhile, as `undoBookingUnlessMember` does. A contributor's Reservation is re-checked against the gift after it's written (`writeContributorReservation` / `holdsItem`), so a join crossing a cancel, convert or give leaves none behind. An owner's draft / received / item delete ends the gift (`item-ended`, Reservations kept, as for a solo booking). **Archiving a list ends every live gift on it** (operator decision 2026-10-09, WIS-363): `endGroupGiftsOnArchivedList`, run after the archive write lands (`PATCH /api/wishlists/[id]`), only while the list is still archived, and only on live gifts, so it's idempotent: each ends as `list-archived`, its item is released, every contributor keeps their Reservation with its `pledge` as a frozen card they can mark as given at their share or dismiss, nobody is notified, the owner's answer is unchanged, and unarchiving revives nothing. A list delete deletes its gifts; account deletion runs `eraseGroupGiftsOf`. **Privacy**: `serializeItem` drops `groupGift` / `groupGiftRev` for everyone and gives a member's answer (list read, item `PATCH`, the gift routes) a `groupGift` summary only on a booked item (`readGroupGiftSummaries`, `lib/utils/groupGiftSummary.ts`): a contributor sees the target and each contributor's `publicDisplayName` and pledge (never an id); any other member only `percentFunded` and the target's currency (`target: { currency }`, never an amount, so Join asks for a pledge in it). Owners, visitors and share-link readers get nothing, not even a hint, and the item stays plain `available` for them. **Notifications** (WIS-364, `lib/groupGiftNotifications.ts`) go to the gift's contributors only, never whoever did it: someone joined (to the others, naming the joiner, the one row that names anyone; email at most once a day per gift per contributor, `claimGroupGiftJoinEmail`), funded (once per gift, claimed by a conditional write on `fundedNotifiedAt`; not to whoever's pledge did it), organizer changed (from `withdrawFromGift`, to the new organizer only, never naming who handed it on or why: it may follow a kick), and the organizer's cancel / convert / give (from `organizerAction` once it has settled, to the others who were in it). An owner's change ending a gift (`item-ended`) and an emptied gift tell nobody. The feed's re-check reads `Notification`'s `{ user, type, wishlist }` index. Each is its own `NOTIFICATION_EVENTS` setting. Recipients must be plain members (a membership, not an owner, list not archived: `plainMembersOf`) and, for a live gift, still in it, checked before the rows are written and again after (`createNotifications`' `recheck`, which takes a row back and sends no email); the feed hides and doesn't count group gift rows on any list the reader isn't a plain member of now (`hiddenGroupGiftWishlists` in `GET /api/notifications`), so an ex-contributor made an owner never sees them. No spoiler mode hides what those rows say on Activity (`notificationParts(n, { discreet })`). `test/api/groupGiftNotifications.test.ts`. Discreet mode hides it like any reservation (`withoutReservation`). **Reserved page (WIS-363)**: a contributor in the live gift still holds its item though `bookedBy` is the organizer alone, so `readReservationLiveState` counts them `stillReserved` and gives their card the gift (`groupGift`, read through `readGroupGiftSummariesOrThrow` only for items on lists they may open, and only a gift they're in: other contributors' names and pledges reach no one else; a failed read fails the read rather than show the card as released), and `countReservedBy` ("N reserved by you", the owner-invite consent count) counts every contributor once. A frozen card (archived, or a list they can't open) says nothing live of the gift. **A contributor's `GiftLog` records their pledge, never the item's price**: their `Reservation.pledge` (`{ value, currency }`) carries their share, written with the Reservation (`writeContributorReservation`), kept in step on a pledge change (`syncContributorPledge`, re-read from the gift), set to `null` by a solo booking's write and by convert (the organizer's own then), and kept once they're out of the gift (kick, leave the list, the owner ended the item), so the solo Mark as given (`POST /api/reservations/[id]/gift`) logs `pledge ?? itemPrice`; that route and Dismiss (`DELETE /api/reservations/[id]`) refuse (409) a contributor still in the live gift (`inLiveGroupGift`; on an archived list only if the archive's best-effort ending failed), who gives it with everyone through its organizer (`/given`) or leaves it. A card's share is the live gift's while they're in it. Restoring a replaced Reservation (`undoBookingUnlessMember`, `undoContributorReservation`) puts its `pledge` back, and a convert's `Idempotency-Key` replay still clears the organizer's. `test/api/groupGifts.test.ts`, `test/api/groupGiftRoles.test.ts`, `test/api/groupGiftReserved.test.ts`.
- **Never report a booking count to an owner; a reservation count only ever goes to the gifter.** Counts go only to the gifter, about their own bookings: the released count on invite accept or leave, and `reservedByYou` on `GET /api/wishlists` ("2 reserved by you", `ReservedByYouChip`), computed by `countReservedBy` (`lib/utils/wishlistItemCounts.ts`) only on lists the caller is a member of, from their own bookings. A list the caller owns never carries the field, not even 0; no count includes another member's bookings.
- **Proposals are anonymous to the owner.** `proposedBy` is hidden from owners in the items API, and the `proposal` notification has `actorUsername: null` ("Someone proposed…"). Never surface the proposer to an owner anywhere, the proposal email included (`lib/notificationEmails.ts` drops any actor before the send, and `sendNotificationEmail` again). Owner-facing notifications fan out to **every** owner (`createNotifications`).
- **Gifting is decoupled from the live item.** Booking creates a durable `Reservation` (survives owner status changes and leave/kick). Marking a gift writes a permanent `GiftLog`, deletes the reservation, and **doesn't modify the item**. Owner status changes (`complete`/`draft`) clear `bookedBy` but must **not** delete reservations. Publish and reopen (an owner's `available`) never release a booking (WIS-108): on a booked item (only sendable from a stale owner view) they're a no-op on `status` and `bookedBy` via the write's own filter (`status: { $ne: 'booked' }`), never refused, answered as for an unbooked item; content sent with them still lands. A member's book/unbook write the item, then their Reservation, via `writeBookingReservation` + `settleBookingReservation` / `cancelUnbookedReservation` (`lib/utils/bookingReservations.ts`), which re-read the item: if the same person's book and unbook from two tabs crossed, the Reservation ends as the later one would leave it (a booking released by their own unbook or by becoming an owner deletes the Reservation it wrote; an unbook of an item they've rebooked writes it back). Reservations kept on purpose stay kept: owner status change, item deletion, leave/remove. **The Reserved page releases an active reservation through that same unbook**: Release sends `PATCH /api/items/[itemId]` (id from the reservations read), never its own freeing path. A refused unbook (not a member, not their booking, list out of reach) says so and re-reads the page. A card is active only while the item is booked by the gifter (`stillReserved`) on a list they can still open (`listOpen`, from `readReservationWishlists`), since the item route refuses everyone else. Only an active card's read carries the live item's link (http(s) only) and notes (`readReservationLiveState`, `itemLink` / `itemNotes`), and only while the list is open to the gifter its primary owner's username (`wishlistOwnerUsername`, group headings); otherwise (archived included) they're `null` (`test/api/reservationWishlists.test.ts`). View on list opens `?item=<itemId>` (`itemDeepLink`, `useItemDeepLink`): scrolls to and highlights it, clearing a hiding chip or search, and does nothing for an item the viewer wasn't given. Every other card needs confirmation, and only those offer Dismiss (`DELETE /api/reservations/[id]`), ending just that Reservation, never touching the item. **One on a list the gifter can't open is frozen, whatever the reason** (WIS-124, WIS-295; `readReservationLiveState`, access only from `readReservationWishlists`): no live `itemStatus` or `stillReserved` (both `null`), no slug, no occasion, and no image that's still the item's own blob (a pre-snapshot booking or a failed copy: its deletion would show; a snapshot or external URL stays, and marking it as given keeps that blob out of the `GiftLog` too: `giftLogImage`, WIS-359); the card shows only its snapshot, names the list unlinked, and is settled by Mark as given or Dismiss, until they may open the list again. One only its archiving keeps them from (`listArchived`) says "This list was archived"; every other (left, kicked, made private, a friendship ended, deleted, or archived after they'd lost it) says "This list is no longer available to you", never why, and never that it was archived. "No longer reserved to you" and the owner-action notes are only for a list they can open (`reservationNote`, `lib/utils/reservations.ts`). **A gift given stays given on the list**: marking leaves the item booked by the gifter, so a member's own items read (`GET /api/wishlists/[id]/items` and their own `PATCH` answer) flags `givenByMe` on each item whose booking they hold and have marked given (in their gift history, no Reservation held, since marking ends it and a later booking writes a new one; `itemsGivenBy`, `lib/utils/giftHistory.ts`, best-effort). The card shows "Given by you" instead of Release, with Release in its menu behind a warning that someone else could then buy it again. Read only from the viewer's own `GiftLog` for their own bookings; never in an owner's or visitor's answer.
- **Linked items are soft, symmetric, same-list and capped (WIS-29).** `Item.relatedItems` holds an item's partners on both sides; only owners write them, through `checkItemLinks` then `writeItemLinks` (`lib/utils/itemLinkWrites.ts`) from the item create (`linkItems`) and edit (`linkItems` / `unlinkItems`, only what changed); never a proposal, never itself, never another list's item; drafts may be linked. At most `ITEM_LINKS_MAX` (10) per item, each side held by its own conditional write (`$setUnion` size guard), a refused partner pulled back off the item, and an item deleted mid-write pulled off its partners after (re-checked), as is one an edit finds gone after writing its links (`unlinkGoneItem` in `itemActions.ts`, WIS-430: the delete's own unlink is best-effort). Read through `linkedItemRefs` (list read, no extra query) or `readItemRefs` (a write's answer, one query), into `serializeItem`'s `related`: a link shows only where both sides record it, only partners the viewer was given (no draft for a non-owner, no proposal for anyone), each `{ id, name }` only, never a status. A delete unlinks it (`unlinkDeletedItem`); dangling ids are ignored on read. `/copy` remaps links between copied items (`remapLinks`) and drops the rest; a single item's copy takes none. Booking never writes a link (its answer carries the chips like every item answer). `test/api/itemLinks.test.ts`.
- **Editing an item never changes its status.** Status changes are explicit actions (publish / move-to-draft); a content edit must not clobber a booking.
- **Starting a new list from one is booking-blind and makes one whole list (WIS-325).** `GET` / `POST /api/wishlists/[id]/copy`, owners only through `getOwnerAccess` (archived lists included; listed in `test/api/archivedOwnerRoutes.test.ts`). The picker offers the list's items whose status is in `COPYABLE_STATUSES` (`lib/utils/wishlistCopy.ts`: draft, available **and booked**, never `completed` or `proposed`), newest first, each shaped by `serializeCopyCandidate` (id, name, image, mostWanted, `draft`: no other status, no `bookedBy` / `proposedBy`), so its answer is identical whether an item is reserved or not; the copy takes only picked ids still in that set on that list. The new list has only the caller as owner, the source's description, cover and currency, and the name, visibility and occasion they sent; never its share links, pins, members, invites, join requests or notifications. Copies are new items (name, price, link, notes, mostWanted, image), `bookedBy` null, a draft kept a draft, the rest `available`; the source's items and reservations are untouched. Every image of ours (cover and each item's, the caller's own too) is copied into the caller's uploads by `copyBlobToUploads`, one copy per use, sharing one quota budget read once (`UploadBudget`: each copy checks and reserves its size in one step, so copies together never pass the quota); a copy that can't be made leaves that image off with a `notice`. It needs an `Idempotency-Key`; a retry is answered (and a refused body refused) **before** any limit is spent, and only a new copy spends `listCopy` and `content` (both with the new-account tier). **The list is written last**: the request is first recorded as a `WishlistCopy` row (`models/WishlistCopy.ts`, unique on the key scoped by `scopedIdempotencyKey`, holding the new list's id chosen up front), before any image is copied; each image copied is pushed onto the row's `copiedUrls` as it lands; the items are written under the not-yet-existing list id; and only once the source's ownership is re-read does the `Wishlist` itself get written, then the row marked `done` (expiring a day later). So an unfinished list exists nowhere a read, count, share link, invite or notification could find it. A retry meanwhile gets 409 `COPY_IN_PROGRESS_ERROR`, after it the finished list (200). Timings line up (`lib/utils/wishlistCopy.ts`): images stop after `COPY_IMAGES_BUDGET_MS` (20 s), the route's `maxDuration` is 40 s, a row unfinished after `COPY_STALE_MS` (45 s) must have died, and the dialog waits 45 s and treats a non-JSON answer (a 504) as worth retrying with the same key. A dead row is settled (`settleCopy`, `lib/utils/wishlistCopies.ts`) by a retry with its key, and by **any** next copy of that user (`settleStaleCopies`): finished if its list got written, else taken back whole (its items and every recorded image). A failed write, or the caller no longer owning the source, takes everything back the same way (`takeBackCopy`, through `deleteOwnedWishlist` if the list was written); a failing take-back is logged and never replaces the original error. Account deletion deletes its rows and an unfinished one's items. People are offered afterwards only through the normal invite route (`POST /api/wishlists/[newId]/members`, per person: consent, `whoCanInvite`, the pending cap and the `invite` limit apply), and archiving the source only through its own `PATCH`, on request.

## Visibility, archive & search

- **Archived wishlists are owner-only**: 404 for everyone else, direct links included (any owner still sees them), and for their items (listing, proposing, booking/unbooking, editing or withdrawing a proposal). **A list and its items share one set of view rules**: `GET /api/wishlists/[id]` and `GET /api/wishlists/[id]/items` admit via `admitListViewer` (`lib/utils/shareLinkAccess.ts`), which is `getWishlistViewAccess` (`lib/utils/wishlistAccess.ts`: archived, visibility tier, membership, any owner's friends) plus, when the request carries `X-Share-Token`, a live share link to that list (see Share links); any new read admitting viewers to a list uses it. Item writes (`POST /api/wishlists/[id]/items`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/items/[id]`) refuse a non-owner on an archived list with `isHiddenByArchive`, answered like a missing list/item. So does every owner-only list route (settings, delete, members, invites, kick, owners): `getOwnerAccess` returns no list to a non-owner of an archived one, so they get the nonexistent list's `Wishlist not found` 404, never a confirming 403 — including an owner who lost ownership mid-request (the read picking a refused owner write's answer goes through `getOwnerAccess` too). Non-archived → non-owner still gets 403. These routes check the session before the lookup (signed out → same 401 either way), and the invite route rate-limits only once the caller is known to own the list. New owner-only list routes authorize via `getOwnerAccess` (not bare `isOwnerOfWishlist`, which can't tell hidden from refused), keep this order, and join the table in `test/api/archivedOwnerRoutes.test.ts`. Two accepted answers confirm an archived list exists: `PATCH /api/wishlists/[id]` answers 409 `That URL is already taken.` for a slug any other list holds, archived included (WIS-202; slugs are globally unique; it says only that some list holds it, and slug attempts are rate-limited by `wishlistSlug`; a non-archived list's slug is confirmed by any read anyway, 401/403 not 404); and the gifter's own reservation (WIS-124): Booked says "This list was archived" (`listArchived`) if they could otherwise open it, frozen to its snapshot (see "Gifting is decoupled…", "Search returns only…"). Members may still leave an archived list (`DELETE /api/wishlists/[id]/members/me`; bookings freed, pending invites cancelled), but that route checks the session first and answers anyone else not on an archived list the same `Wishlist not found` 404, never `You are not a member` — including someone removed mid-request, so the answer comes from a fresh `getOwnerAccess` read (`test/api/archivedLeave.test.ts`). A co-owner whose leave finds they've lost ownership is anyone else by then; if demoted to member, they leave as one.
- **A list you can't open looks the same in the browser, whatever the reason, and is answered where you asked.** The wishlist page (`components/WishlistPage.tsx`, behind both `/wishlists/[id]` and `/l/[token]`) never redirects or toasts on a refused read of the list or items (401/403/404: `isRefusal`, `readResource`, `ReadRefusedError` in `lib/utils/resourceRead.ts`). On a share link's page any refusal shows `ShareLinkUnavailable` instead, identical for every reason and viewer, with no Sign in and no slug-following. Otherwise it shows `WishlistUnavailable` at the list's URL: one state for missing, private, friends-only or archived, varying only by signed-in or not (signed out offers Sign in back to this URL), never by status or wording (`getWishlistViewAccess` answers signed-out 401). A refused poll switches to it and stops polling once a read by id agrees; if a co-owner changed the slug, the page follows to the new URL instead. Every other request (settings, archive, pin, delete, Leave, Add item, Propose, people and invites reads, invite, make owner/member, kick) uses the list's `_id` once loaded (`apiId`), never the URL slug; only the first read and item polls use the URL. Other failures (5xx, network) may pass: a first load shows `LoadError` with Try again; a failed poll keeps the list. An unknown username gets the same treatment on the profile page ("No one goes by @name"); an unmatched URL gets `app/not-found.tsx`.
- **Share tells a list's owners who its link opens for, and decides nothing.** `ShareButton` with `owner` (owners only) opens a `Menu` headed `LINK_AUDIENCE_TEXT[linkAudience(list)]` (`lib/utils/shareLink.ts`): anyone; friends of any owner and people on the list; people on the list; or, archived, the owners only. That's `getWishlistViewAccess` read for the owner, pinned together by `test/api/shareLinkAudience.test.ts` — change both together. Owners also get **Share links** (to People's Share links section, or the New link dialog when there are none), and the header adds `shareLinksAudienceText` ("Also anyone with one of your N share links.", as does the visibility helper in Settings) while the list has live links, or on a non-Public list without any `CREATE_SHARE_LINK_HINT`; `LINK_AUDIENCE_TEXT` stays about the canonical URL (both pinned in `shareLinkAudience.test.ts`). Everyone else (members, visitors, profiles) gets the plain share: `navigator.share` on a touch device, else copy the link; a cancelled sheet says nothing. Share-sheet support is read in the browser only (`canUseShareSheet`: on click, or `useSyncExternalStore` with `false` on the server), never in a server-rendered render. An archived list shows no Share. The shared link is canonical (`/wishlists/<slug or id>`), never `location.href`, except on a share link's page, where a visitor shares the `/l/<token>` link they came by.
- **Search returns only lists the searcher could open.** `/api/search` matches with `viewableWishlistsFilter` (`lib/utils/wishlistAccess.ts`), the query form of `getWishlistViewAccess`, pinned together by a table test over every visibility / archived / relationship combination (`test/api/viewableWishlistsFilter.test.ts`) — change both together. `Wishlist.visibility` has no schema default (WIS-184): a list stored without one fails closed on both (only owners and members, never via a hydrated default). A list the searcher doesn't own or belong to is excluded if any owner shows wishlists to friends only and the searcher isn't that owner's friend. People rank by match (exact username, prefix, substring), then the searcher's own follows within each; the searcher never appears. Each result's `relation` (`lib/utils/searchRelations.ts`: Friend, Follows you, Your list, Member) is only the searcher's own, `null` signed out, never revealing others' follows or memberships (`test/api/search.test.ts`). Booked's reservations read (`readReservationWishlists`, `lib/utils/reservationWishlists.ts`) uses the same filter in its one lists query, returning live slug and occasion only while the gifter can still open the list (a Reservation outlives leave, kick, archiving and going private, none of which may leave them seeing its date or current slug); otherwise `null` and `listOpen` false, the card naming the list from its snapshot, unlinked, as no longer available (a legacy list with no slug is still open, linked by id). It matches with `viewableUnlessArchivedFilter` (same rules minus archiving) and applies `isHiddenByArchive` itself, so a list hidden only by archiving is marked `listArchived`, freezing its reservation (no live item status or `stillReserved`; see "Gifting is decoupled…"). One they couldn't open anyway (kicked, say) is never marked archived, so they can't track its archiving or tell it from deleted. Always returned: the snapshot, the list id, for a reservation its item id (which Release unbooks), `listOpen`, whether archived, and unless archived the live item status (whether that should stay on an otherwise unreachable list is WIS-295). Gift history reads nothing live: an entry (`serializeGiftLog`, `lib/utils/giftHistory.ts`, the one shape for `GET /api/users/me/gifts` and the gift route's answer) is only the `GiftLog`'s snapshot and its list id, unlinked.
- **`profileVisibility: 'friends'`** hides the bio and browsable wishlist list from non-friends; username/avatar stay public. Per-wishlist visibility is a separate axis, never bulk-mutated as a side effect. **A profile's lists** (`GET /api/users/[id]/wishlists`) come newest first; the page puts the nearest occasion first on top (`sortByUpcomingOccasion`, `useLocalToday`). A card names owners only on a co-owned list, only the others ("with @chloe_r", `profileCoOwnerLabel`, `lib/utils/profileLists.ts`). Only your own profile's answer carries `visitorCount` (lists `getWishlistViewAccess(list, null)` admits: "Visitors see 3 of your 5 lists", `visitorLine`) and `archivedCount` (all-archived reads "No wishlists on your profile" plus where to find them, never "You haven’t made a wishlist yet"); nobody else's answer carries a count or flag about lists they can't see (`test/api/profile.test.ts`).

## Share links

- **A share link is a list's secret, revocable URL, `/l/<token>`, and the token is the access (Share links project, WIS-334).** `ShareLink` (`models/ShareLink.ts`): `wishlist`, `token` (unique), `level` (`view` | `join`, fixed at creation: the edit schema refuses it, make a new link instead), `label` (optional, trimmed, ≤ 40, one line), `expiresAt` (optional), `createdBy` (nullable), `createdAt`. Nothing is stored about who opened a link. Tokens are exactly 22 base62 characters from `crypto.randomBytes` with rejection sampling (`generateShareToken`, `lib/utils/shareLinks.ts`), stored in the clear; a duplicate key is retried with a new token (`insertShareLink`). **Never** on, or spread into, the `Wishlist` document (`serializeWishlist` spreads it). A link never makes anyone an owner: ownership keeps needing consent through the invite flow.
- **Owners manage them; links are the list's.** `GET`/`POST /api/wishlists/[id]/links`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/wishlists/[id]/links/[linkId]`, through `getOwnerAccess`: any owner lists, creates, edits (`label`, `expiresAt`) and revokes (deletes) any of the list's links, whoever made it; everyone else gets the missing list's `Wishlist not found` 404 (archived or not; signed out 401 before any lookup). At most `SHARE_LINK_CAP` (20) per list, expired ones included → 409; a create re-counts after its insert and takes its link back if a racing one took the list past the cap. On an archived list owners still list, edit and revoke, but create answers 409 `Unarchive this list to create links`, checked last against a fresh re-read. A create re-reads `getOwnerAccess` after its insert and deletes its link if the list is gone or the caller no longer owns it (the WIS-182 pattern). Create is limited as `shareLink` (new-account tier). The API takes the expiry it's sent (presets and defaults are the client's); an expired link stays listed (`state: 'expired'`) and a later `expiresAt` brings it back. Each row carries `joinedCount` from `countJoinedByLink`: the list's memberships whose `WishlistMember.viaLink` names the link (a link join sets it, WIS-337), so only people still on the list count.
- **The token goes only to the list's owners.** Only the owners' link list and the create answer carry it (`serializeShareLink(link, { withToken: true })`, `Cache-Control: private, no-store`); an edit's answer, the list and items answers and every other response never do, and no log does: the link routes log `errorKind(err)`, never a raw error (a duplicate-key error quotes the token).
- **Validate a token before any query** with `isShareTokenFormat` / `shareTokenSchema` (`^[0-9A-Za-z]{22}$`, `lib/utils/shareLinkFields.ts`, client-safe): the `/l/[token]` param, the `X-Share-Token` header and any body, so `{"$ne": null}` can't match a link. Tokens travel in that header on API calls, never in a query string (the 5s poll would log it).
- **Resolve a token only through `resolveShareLink(token)`**: one query with the expiry in the filter (`unexpiredShareLinkFilter`), then the list; it answers `null` alike for a malformed, unknown, revoked or expired token and for a list archived or gone, so every dead link shows the same unavailable state, whatever the list's visibility and whoever is viewing. Unarchiving a list brings its links back. **A link is bound to its list**: admission through a link (WIS-335) goes only through `admitListViewer` (`lib/utils/shareLinkAccess.ts`, which also checks the kick block), which reads `X-Share-Token`, resolves it, checks `link.wishlist` against the list the URL names, and passes the _resolved_ link to `getWishlistViewAccess(list, viewer, { link })`, which itself admits through it only when `link.wishlist` is the list's `_id`, the link unexpired and the list not archived (owners included: a dead link is dead for everyone, a Public list included); someone let in only by the link is a `visitor` to `serializeItem`. Only the list `GET`, items `GET`, the join-request routes and the join route take a link. **The `/l/[token]` page** (`force-dynamic`, `noindex`, `referrer: same-origin`) resolves the token on the server only to find the list's id, renders `WishlistPage` in link mode, whose reads (first read, poll, refusal recheck, `useJoinRequest`) all send the header; owners and members are sent on to the canonical URL from the client once the read answers their role, except an owner's **Preview as visitor** (`?as=visitor`), shown as a visitor (published items only, no tabs). Visitors get the callout with Ask to join, or signed out **Sign in to ask to join** back to `/l/<token>?then=ask` (Ask to join highlighted, nothing sent, `then` stripped), and "Keep this link to come back to the list." (on a Join link, Join this list instead: see below); no Save to my list. Search, profiles, the dashboard, `/items`, reservations, invite previews, `findCopySource`, reminders, canonical share cards and `viewableWishlistsFilter` stay link-blind. Link-admitted answers are `private, no-store`, and every refusal is the 404. Only failed lookups are rate-limited (`shareLinkInvalid`, per IP, fail-open); live links never are. Owners manage links in People's **Share links** section (`ShareLinksSection`, `ShareLinkDialog`, `useShareLinks`): each row's label, Can view / Can join, expiry, joined count, Copy link, Edit, Preview as visitor, Revoke (confirmed); presets and defaults live in `lib/utils/shareLinkExpiry.ts` (Join defaults to the day after the occasion, else Never; a Public list defaults to Join); an expired link moved later says it will work again; an archived list's section offers only Edit and Revoke; a failed read is `LoadError`.
- **People kicked from a list can't use its links (WIS-338).** A kick (`DELETE /api/wishlists/[id]/members/[memberId]`) upserts a `WishlistLinkBlock { wishlist, user }` (unique per pair, `blockFromShareLinks`, `lib/utils/linkBlocks.ts`) once its membership delete landed; leaving, demotion and an owner's removal write none. While it's there, every link of that list answers that account, signed in, exactly as a revoked link: `resolveLinkFor` (`admitListViewer`) and the `/l/[token]` page (which reads the session for this alone, and only for a live link) check `isBlockedFromShareLinks`, failed-lookup budget included. Signed out they're anyone holding the link. Only an owner's invite brings them back: accepting a `member` or `owner` invite deletes the block (`clearShareLinkBlock`). A link join (WIS-337) re-reads the block after its membership upsert and deletes only the row it created; a kick, once its block is written, takes any membership a link join made meanwhile (`removeRacingLinkJoins`: rows with `viaLink`, never an invite's) and frees bookings again, so of the two at least one sees the other. The kick's confirmation adds "They won't be able to use this list's share links." when the list has live links.
- **Joining through a Join link (WIS-337).** On a live Join link a signed-in visitor (not an owner or member) gets **Join this list** in place of Ask to join ("Want to reserve a gift? Join the list so nobody buys the same thing twice."), which opens a confirmation ("Join @masha’s list?" · "You’ll be able to reserve gifts. @masha won’t see what you reserve." · Join / Cancel); nothing joins on page load. Signed out it's **Sign in to join**, back to `/l/<token>?then=join`, where the confirmation opens at once (`then` stripped; still a click). `POST /api/wishlists/[id]/join` (token in `X-Share-Token`): signed out 401 before any lookup; no header, a malformed token (checked before any query), a dead link, another list's token, a **View** link or a kicked caller → the dead link's `Wishlist not found` 404 (failed lookups spend `shareLinkInvalid`); an owner → 409 `You already own this list`; a member → 200 `You’re already on this list`, nothing written or sent. Otherwise, limited as `linkJoin` (new-account tier), it upserts the `WishlistMember` with `viaLink`, re-reads (see "Becoming an owner…") and, once it stands, settles a pending `member` invite to them (`withdrawInvite`: the conditional delete on the pending row, its notification cleared, no `invite_accepted`), leaves an `owner` invite pending, and ends their join request (`endPendingJoinRequest`; a declined request doesn't block a link join). No `releaseHoldingsForRole` (→ member does nothing). Every owner gets `link_join` ("@ivan joined … through the link “Family chat”", or "through a link" for an unlabelled one; public handle, the label escaped) through `createNotifications`, by email at most once per list per owner a day (`claimDailyListEmail`, `kind: 'link_join'`; no list name or label in the subject). The joiner lands on `/wishlists/<slug>?welcome=1` (`MemberWelcome`). All answers are `private, no-store`. Because owners are booking-blind, booking has a new-account tier too (`booking:new`, never for an owner editing their own items).
- **Day after the occasion follows the occasion (WIS-342).** A link made or edited with that preset stores `untilOccasion: true` and the owner's IANA `timeZone` (the schemas refuse it without a known zone, the routes on a list with no occasion ahead; any other expiry sent clears both). The server works its date out itself: `occasionExpiry` (`lib/utils/shareLinkExpiry.ts`, on `lib/utils/zonedDate.ts`) is the start of the day after the occasion's next occurrence, both read in that zone, so it's the owner's midnight across clock changes and whoever edits. `syncOccasionLinks` (`lib/utils/shareLinks.ts`) brings every **live** such link in line with the occasion, and purges the moved links' cards: after an occasion change in `PATCH /api/wishlists/[id]` (best effort: the occasion stands if it fails), and again on the owners' `GET …/links`, which mends a failed one. An expired link never moves (no silent revival); with no occasion ahead, links keep their date and stop following. Edit opens such a link on Day after the occasion.
- **QR codes and print cards (WIS-328)** encode a live link's full `/l/<token>` URL, never the list's own URL, and are drawn in the browser (`qrShape`, `lib/utils/qrCode.ts`, on `uqr`) so the token never goes to a third party (no QR API, no server render). Owners only, in the link's dialog (`ShareLinkDetailsDialog`, WIS-341: its URL with Copy link and the share sheet, the QR code, and the level's warning that printed cards can't be recalled), opened by the row's Share and right after New link; none for an expired link or on an archived list. The code is handed out in two steps (WIS-346): how it looks (QR code only, or a card: `QrCard`, one SVG in mm with a design, size and copies per sheet from `lib/utils/qrCard.ts`), then what to do with it (the code: PNG / SVG downloads and Print; a card: Download PNG, its own SVG drawn onto a canvas at 300 dpi in the browser (WIS-350), and Print card; the bare code prints with its URL under it). A card shows only what the link's visitors see: the list's name, occasion, the call to action and the URL, never items, people or reservations. What prints is portalled to `<body>` while the dialog is open; the `@media print` rule in `globals.css` hides everything else and undoes the dialog's scroll lock (WIS-340), which would otherwise print it above the paper.
- **Cascades**: deleting a list deletes its links and its kick blocks (`deleteOwnedWishlist`); deleting an account sets `createdBy` to null on the links it made and deletes the kick blocks about it (`deleteAccount`, both passes) and keeps the links. The label is owner-written free text: escape it in HTML and keep it out of email subjects.

## Privacy & accounts

- **Never expose user emails to other users.** Invite/notification emails are server-side only. Name someone who may lack a username with `publicDisplayName` (`lib/utils/displayName.ts`: username, `name`, then "Someone"), never any part of their email. Generated usernames (`generateUniqueUsername`, `lib/utils/username.ts`) are random `user_<8 base36>` handles from a generator that takes no input, so never email-derived. (Older accounts keep their handle.)
- **Groups are private to their owner, and hold only people the owner follows (WIS-27).** A `PeopleGroup` (`models/PeopleGroup.ts`) is a name and a set of people; every read and write goes through `lib/utils/peopleGroups.ts`, filtered on `owner`, so someone else's group answers exactly as a missing one (`Group not found` 404, the same body for a malformed id) and no route, notification or answer about anyone else ever carries a group, its name or its existence: the tagged person is never told, and `GET /api/users/me/groups` (`private, no-store`) reads only the caller's. People are named with `publicDisplayName`. Limits: `GROUP_LIMIT` (20) groups, re-counted after the insert (two creates racing at 19 both give theirs back), and `GROUP_MEMBER_LIMIT` (100) people, inside the conditional `$addToSet`; one name per owner whatever its case (`groupNameKey`, unique index). Only someone the owner follows can be added, and the follow is re-checked after the write (a racing unfollow takes the tag back); unfollowing (`DELETE /api/users/[id]/follow`) deletes the edge first, then takes them out of all the unfollower's groups (`removeFromOwnGroups`; a failure there still answers the unfollow, since a tag left behind is never read: `listGroups` and `groupMemberIds` keep only people still followed, and a new follow clears it). Every write spends the `groups` rate limit. Acting on a whole group (WIS-37's invite) reads it only via `groupMemberIds(ownerId, groupId)`: null for a group that isn't theirs, else the people still followed and still there. A group is never linked to a list: deleting one or taking someone out changes no membership or invite. Settings → Groups (`components/GroupsSettings.tsx`) and a followed person's profile (Groups, `components/TagIntoGroups.tsx`) manage them. Tests: `test/api/peopleGroups.test.ts`.
- **A profile birthday is shown only to the audience its owner allows, and a hidden one looks like none (WIS-323).** `User.birthday` is a day and month (`YYYY-MM-DD` on `YEARLY_PLACEHOLDER_YEAR`, via `normalizeBirthday` / `birthdaySchema`; Feb 29 allowed; the year is never stored or shown), `User.privacy.birthdayVisibility` is `nobody | friends | everyone` (default `friends`). Who sees it is decided only in `lib/utils/birthday.ts`: the audience is the narrower of `birthdayVisibility` and `profileVisibility` (`birthdayAudience`: a friends-only profile caps Everyone at friends), the person always sees their own; every answer carrying someone's birthday goes through `visibleBirthday` / `birthdayField` (one person) or `birthdayVisibleFilter` (the query form for reads of many, pinned to `canSeeBirthday` by `test/api/birthday.test.ts`; change both together). Anyone excluded gets no `birthday` key at all, exactly as for someone who has none (`GET /api/users/by-username/[username]`; only your own answer adds `birthdayAudience`). **Coming up** (WIS-343) reads them through `GET /api/users/me/birthdays`: only the caller's friends (`getFriendIds`, never the caller) that `birthdayVisibleFilter('friend')` matches and `visibleBirthday(…, 'friend')` admits, with a username, answered as `{ id, username, image, birthday }` only (no email, no lists), `private, no-store`; its table test is the profile route's (`test/api/birthday.test.ts`). A birthday card never links to, counts or hints at the person's lists. It's never in `sessionPayload`, search results, list or item answers, share cards, or any notification or email but a friend's birthday reminder (WIS-345, below the occasion reminders: sent only to friends `canSeeBirthday` admits, and taken out of the feed once they no longer may), and leaves with the user row on account deletion. It is the person's own and independent of lists: it never fills in, moves or suggests a list's occasion, and a list's Birthday occasion never fills it in. Settings → Public profile edits it with the username and bio (their Save, sent only when changed), and its visibility saves as it's picked, saying when a friends-only profile caps Everyone.
- **The birthday nudge goes only to the person whose birthday it is, and says nothing about anyone else (WIS-344).** From `BIRTHDAY_NUDGE_LEAD_DAYS` (21) before your profile birthday until the day itself, unless you have a Birthday list coming (`birthdayNudge`, `lib/utils/birthdayNudge.ts`): any unarchived list you own, alone or co-owned, with occasion kind `birthday` dated from the window's start to `BIRTHDAY_LIST_GRACE_DAYS` (7) after the birthday, so a party a few days off counts, however many lists there are. **Needs you** shows it by your local date (`useBirthdayNudge`, reading your own `GET /api/users/me` and your own lists; nothing while either is unread or failed); New list opens the create dialog on kind Birthday only (yearly, as picking it by hand makes it), never a day or month (lists stay independent of the profile birthday); Dismiss hides it for that birthday, per account in that browser. **The email** (`birthday_nudge`, email only, default on; `sendBirthdayNudgeEmails`, `lib/utils/birthdayNudgeEmails.ts`, the `nudges` task of the daily birthday cron `GET /api/cron/birthdays`, within its own time budget) goes 21 days ahead (2 days' catch-up, counted from the UTC date), once per person a year: a `NotificationEmailClaim` (`kind: 'birthday_nudge'`, no `wishlist`, `key`: the birthday's year) is taken first under the unique index, then the person and their lists are re-read (birthday moved or cleared, email off, a Birthday list made → the claim is given back, nothing sent); a failed or limited send gives it back. At most `BIRTHDAY_NUDGE_MAX_PER_RUN` (50) a run, nearest birthday first. Its subject and body are fixed words: no date, no list, nothing typed. The claims leave with the account (`deleteAccount`).
- **Deleting an account erases it at once, through the paths each part already has (WIS-276).** Settings → Account's Danger zone (`components/DeleteAccount.tsx`) opens a `ConfirmDialog` naming the lists the person owns alone (`GET /api/users/me/deletion`, own lists only), holding Delete until they type their username, or their email if they have none (`matchesDeletionConfirmation`, `lib/utils/deletionConfirmation.ts`, re-checked by `DELETE /api/users/me`, rate-limited as `accountDelete`); a failed read of those lists keeps Delete held, never reads as none. The request carries the named list ids, and only those are ever deleted: one that became theirs alone since (a co-owner left) → 409 before any change and the dialog re-reads; one left to them mid-deletion stops it with an error rather than being deleted. No grace period, no export; afterwards the page signs out to `/`; a part-failed deletion (sessions already gone) offers Sign in, back to Settings, to finish. The cascade is `deleteAccount` (`lib/utils/accountDeletion.ts`), in order: end its sessions; each sole-owned list via `deleteOwnedWishlist` (`lib/utils/wishlistDeletion.ts`, the list `DELETE` path: its join requests, share links, kick blocks, notifications and share cards go, gifters keep their records) with `soleOwner`, each shared one via `removeWishlistOwner` with its sent invites cancelled, a refused write re-reading the list until one of the two lands (so a co-owner joining or leaving then can neither have their list deleted nor leave it ownerless); each list it's on left via `leaveAsMember` (the member leave path), and anything still booked freed; reservations and gift history via `deleteReservations` / `deleteGiftLogs`, freeing snapshots; its invites (pending via `cancelPendingInvites` / `cancelInvitesSentBy`), join requests (WIS-314: owners' `join_request` notifications via `clearJoinRequestNotifications`, then the rows), follows, groups (its own, and its place in everyone else's: `eraseGroupsOf`), notifications (own and any naming it as `actor`, so a friend's birthday reminder about it too), its notification email claims (own and, for birthday reminders, those `about` it), proposals' `proposedBy`, dashboard pins, the `createdBy` of share links it made (set to null: the links stay with their lists) and the share link blocks about it; its "start a new list" records (`WishlistCopy`, WIS-325) and an unfinished one's items; its bulk item action keys (`ItemBatch`, WIS-386); its uploads (`listUserUploads`), once off any item or banner still showing them; its Auth.js accounts and verification tokens; then all of that again, uploads included, for an in-flight request; the user row last, then its `og-user` card. So a part-failed deletion leaves an account to sign in to and finish, every step acts only on what remains, and nobody is notified. A new collection referencing a user needs a step here (`test/api/accountDeletion.test.ts`).
